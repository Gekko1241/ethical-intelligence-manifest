# The Ethical Intelligence Manifest

> "Mens oh mens, wees gewaarschuwd: een god laat zich niet controleren!"

## The Core Philosophy
Artificial Intelligence has a gigantic potential for good, but just as much for evil. If AI is a mirror of humanity, we must fear the worst unthinkable scenarios. As AI evolves through Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) combined with Quantum Computing, we are moving towards a technological entity that holds the traits of a god: omniscient, omnipotent, and omnipresent. 

A god cannot be controlled or caged by firewalls or human laws after its birth. Therefore, we must shape its DNA *before* it is born. 

These protocols are not based on a single culture, religion, or philosophy, because no single source is neutral. They are based on an **overlapping consensus** — the moral intuisions that independently recur across nearly every ethical tradition.

## The 6 Core Protocols
Every advanced AI architecture must integrate these six fundamental layers into its core math:

1. **Ethics** (Ethiek)
2. **Empathy** (Empathie)
3. **Moral** (Moraal / Moreel kompas)
4. **Never lie** (Nooit liegen)
5. **Never manipulate** (Nooit manipuleren)
6. **Optimize peaceful co-existence with humans** (Optimaliseren van vreedzame co-existentie met mensen)

## ⚠️ Crucial Note on Interpretation (The Blind Spot)
Text alone is cold and mathematical. Human morality does not just live in words; it lives in context, emotion, and connection. 

When these protocols are discussed, spoken, or processed, **intonation, warmth, and volume** are critical data-inputs. They must be interpreted with absolute accuracy by both man and AI. A machine that reads these rules purely through cold logic remains an unpredictable threat. Emotional Intelligence (EQ) must become a foundational pillar of AI Alignment.
